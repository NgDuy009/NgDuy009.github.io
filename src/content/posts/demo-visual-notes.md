## Begin with the silhouette

An editorial image can be read through its broad shapes before its individual details. Notice where the outline feels quiet, where it changes direction, and how the subject sits within the frame.

This is a general visual-reading exercise, not a review of a real outfit or a claim about a personal collection.

## Notice texture and contrast

Texture gives the eye a reason to pause. A smooth surface beside a visibly woven one can create contrast without relying on a dramatic color palette.

- **Shape:** observe the outline and proportion.
- **Texture:** compare the visual surfaces.
- **Contrast:** note the differences in light, tone, or scale.
